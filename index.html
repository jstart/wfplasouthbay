About Us

Organizing the South Bay

The Working Families Party fights for economic, racial, and climate justice — workers over bosses, people over the powerful. We organize on top of and around the two-party system to elect champions for working families and win bold policies that put consumers and communities first.

WFP LA South Bay is a local organizing hub within the California Working Families Party's Los Angeles region. We're part of a statewide multiracial coalition of grassroots organizations and labor unions, building real governing power from school boards to Congress.

From rising rents to corporate price gouging, South Bay families deserve leaders with the backbone to fight for health care and housing for all, fully funded schools, and a California that works for everyone — not just the wealthy and well-connected.
